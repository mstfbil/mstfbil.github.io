here's what i build

cellmaCE

a cell machine mod for the ti-84 plus ce calculator

if it exists, it's ported to a calculator. and cell machine by sam hogan definitely exists. so i woke up one day and chose violence. you can check it out here, but note that it's still indev.

arrayshift();

was my entry to GMTK Game Jam 2025

in this game, your objective is moving the player through the grid to the goal. except you can shift the grid and shape the level as you wish, because most levels are impossible to complete without doing that.

you can play arrayshift(); on itch.io.

The Passing of Midnight

was my entry to GMTK Game Jam 2026

you are kidnapped and put in a mysterious room with nothing but an old clock in it. you're supposed to nudge the clock in the right ways to only delay the inevitable.

you can play The Passing of Midnight on itch.io.

lipuCE

a toki pona dictionary for when you're bored in class

lipuCE is a toki pona dictionary that runs on ti-84 plus ce. it includes all the core words and has releases that include up to obscure words. you can view word definitions. it's still in development.

you can download it from here.

lovesid

a SID chip emulator for the awesome LÖVE framework

SID chip (i.e. 6581) is the sound generator chip that powers the commodore 64. i made a slightly accurate emulator for it that runs on love2d, so maybe one day somebody can build a full commodore 64 emulator on it.

you can check out the repo from here.
